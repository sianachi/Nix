    October 2026: queries, charts and a faster editor

    6 October 20261 min read
    • release
    • sites

    This release adds saved queries across the whole workspace, more chart types and a much quicker editor on long documents.

    • Saved queries can filter every item you can read, not just one item's children.

    • Charts gain line, area, pie and a year grid.

    • Opening a 20,000-word document is about three times faster.

    Fixes#

    • Calendar events keep their time zone when dragged across a daylight-saving change.

    • Locked items no longer show their children in search previews.
