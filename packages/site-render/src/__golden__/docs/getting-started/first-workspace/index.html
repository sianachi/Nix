    Your first workspace

    Updated 6 October 2026

    A workspace is the top of a tree. Everything you make lives somewhere in it, and its members share it.

    Make a few items#

    1. Press N to create an item, and give it a title.

    2. Type / in its body to add a heading, a checklist or a table.

    3. Drag another item onto it to make it a child.

    Give the children a shape#

    Open the item's views and add a board. Add a Status property, and each child becomes a card you can drag between columns. Switch the same view to a calendar and the cards land on their dates.
