    Collaboration and offline

    Updated 6 October 2026
    Status
    • Stable

    Edits sync live between everyone in a workspace and keep working without a connection.

    Live editing#

    Every document is a shared, conflict-free structure. Two people typing in the same paragraph both keep their words.

    Offline#

    The app keeps a local copy of what you have opened. Edits made offline are queued and merged in order when the connection returns.
