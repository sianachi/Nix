Software engineer in Birmingham, UK, working on IoT, edge computing, backends and applied AI with .NET, Python, TypeScript and Go.

I am a software engineer in Birmingham. Most of my work sits where software meets hardware and data: devices at the edge, the backends they report to, and the models that make sense of what they send.

Day to day that means .NET and Go for services, Python for data and models, and TypeScript for anything with a screen. I like systems that are small enough to understand and boring enough to trust.

Below are the projects I spend my evenings on and the notes I write along the way. If something here is useful to you, I would like to hear about it.
