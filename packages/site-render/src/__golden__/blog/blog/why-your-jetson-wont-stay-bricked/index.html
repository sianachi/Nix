Why Your Jetson Won't Stay Bricked (Probably)

14 September 20261 min read
  • edge
  • hardware
  • iot

The first time a Jetson refuses to boot after a bad flash, it feels final. It almost never is. The board keeps a recovery path in its boot ROM that no update can overwrite.

Recovery mode#

  1. Power the board off.

  2. Hold the recovery button and power it on.

  3. Connect it to a Linux host over USB.

lsusb | grep -i nvidia
sudo ./flash.sh jetson-orin-nano-devkit internal

Warning

Flash from a host running the exact JetPack release you are installing. Mixing versions is the usual cause of a second brick.

The boot chain, briefly#

Diagram source
flowchart LR
  ROM --> MB1 --> MB2 --> UEFI --> Kernel

Once you know the chain, a failure stops being mysterious: the serial console tells you which stage stopped.
