Kubernetes made my life easier

19 July 20261 min read
  • devops
  • engineering

I resisted Kubernetes for my side projects for years. Too much machinery for three services. Then I ran three services by hand for a year.

What changed#

  • Restarts, rollouts and health checks stopped being scripts I maintained.

  • Every service declares what it needs in one place.

  • Moving to a new machine became a matter of pointing the cluster at it.

apiVersion: apps/v1
kind: Deployment
metadata:
  name: jobbie-api
spec:
  replicas: 2

It is still too much for a single static site. It is about right for anything with a database and a worker.
