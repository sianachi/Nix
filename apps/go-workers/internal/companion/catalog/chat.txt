Section: View kinds
Columns may include title; it is an item-title marker, never a form field.
- List (list): optional: groupBy (select, checkbox or $type). Rows and columns, one row per child, optionally in sections.
- Board (board): needs a select property. Cards grouped into columns by a single select property.
- Calendar (calendar): needs a date-shaped property. Items placed on a day, week or month by a date property.
- Timeline (timeline): needs a date-shaped property; optional: endDate (date-shaped). Bars across a time axis; without an end date an item is a milestone.
- Gallery (gallery): optional: cover (image), columns (field keys). Cards with an optional picture and chosen fields.
- Spreadsheet (sheet). Children as an editable grid, one row per child and one column per property.
- Form (form). A single-page fillable form over the schema; each submission is a new child.
- Interactive form (interactive_form). A multi-page, conditional form whose answers create a child item.
- Smart list (query). Saved query rows show filter fields; columns are not configurable.
- Chart (chart): needs a select-or-date property; optional: measureField (number), splitBy (select-or-checkbox). Children counted or totalled per select value, or per period of a date (set period). chartKind: bar, column, pie, or line/area/year on a date; splitBy makes series.
- Habit tracker (habit_tracker). Child habit settings and dated check-ins, not column mappings.
- Checklist (checklist): optional: doneProperty (checkbox). A box to tick per child, with progress; first property column appears beside title.
- Matrix (matrix): needs a select or checkbox property; optional: rowBy (select or checkbox, required). Cards in a grid of cells, placed by two properties at once.
- Outline (outline). The subtree as a collapsible tree of titles, reshaped by keyboard.

Section: Recipes
board, timeline, gallery, sheet, form, interactive-form, query, calendar, list, checklist, matrix, outline, habit-tracker. Each sets up the view kind it names; query is a smart list.
